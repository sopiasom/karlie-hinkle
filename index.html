Systems analysis

Why do the same problems keep coming back?

Most performance problems start in the system around people. I look at three levels:

  1. 1The organizationAre goals and priorities clear, and do they line up across teams?
  2. 2The processDo the steps and handoffs between people actually connect?
  3. 3The people doing the workDoes each person know what's expected, have what they need, have the skills, and get feedback that makes doing it right worth it?

Training only fixes the skills piece. I find where the problem really is, then fix that.

How I work

Practice and feedback,
built into every training.

Most training stops at telling people what to do. Mine has them do it, with coaching, until they get it right.

Step 1
Instruct
I break each skill into clear, specific steps.
Step 2
Model
I show each step done well, so people know what right looks like.
Step 3
Rehearse
People practice in realistic situations and get feedback on the spot.
Step 4
Measure
I collect data before and after, so you can see what changed.
